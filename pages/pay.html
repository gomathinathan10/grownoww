Frequently Asked Questions

Which payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major payment methods across India and internationally, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Diners), Debit Cards, NetBanking across 50+ banks, and corporate credit cards.
Is my payment transaction secure?
Yes, absolutely. All payments are encrypted using bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption and routed directly through Razorpay, a certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateway. GrowNoww does not store any sensitive card numbers or PINs.
Will I receive an official receipt or GST invoice?
Yes! Immediately upon payment completion, you can view and download your official digital transaction receipt. Our accounts team will also issue an official GST tax invoice directly to your registered billing email within 24 hours.
Can I pay for custom milestones or retainers?
Yes. You can enter any custom amount in the input box corresponding to your agreed project proposal or milestone quote, select your service, and add your invoice/reference number.
