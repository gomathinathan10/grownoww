Discovery

Studio Emerge reached out to us with a requirement of building a brand-new website which needed to be a communicative kind of website. Though Studio emerge was in a field for 10 years, but they didn't have a simple & neat website for their detailing and architectural business. With having a decade of experience, they wanted to showcase their vision to be communicated on their website, as they sought to their impact on quality and justice for their detailing works. Besides needing to communicate their vision, the site itself needed a makeover. We went for multiple interviews with their directors and dove deep into the UI/UX research and usability testing. For the usability testing, we used a tool that allowed us to observe anonymous website visits so we could see what people were going on to the site, where they were getting lost or confused, and what issues they ran into.

UX Strategy

After compelling all the data from interviews, audits & studies, our research team analyzed everything. We pulled from what we learned during our deep dive into data to build out the user experience strategy (UX) for the design.

The predominant thing we focused on was the website sitemap which simplified the user flow to look for the information. While executing the sitemap itself we were in the review with the client for the content strategy & obviously with the help of content developer.

After sitemap and content strategy falls in a place, we started working on wireframes which helps to structure the layout of the website. Also wireframes are predominantly used to settle the user journey and we know it is a collaborative process wireframing gave the team at Studio Emerge an accurate preview at how the new site was structured and allowed us to iterate rapidly together to create an ideal user flow.

Build

Once the structural planning was in place, we were ready for the fun part - design! Design is where things come to life as we pulled together the colors, images, fonts, and iconography that would best represent who The Studio Emerge is.

From the technology side of things, we chose to implement our custom site build on the extremely user friendly PHP Laravel. Everything was customized and structured on the backend of the site in a way that empowers the team at Studio Emerge to keep the site fresh and up-to-date.

The front-end development of the site utilizes the latest HTML/CSS and was coded on a responsive framework to ensure an optimal experience for website visitors on devices of all sizes and shapes. It doesn't matter if someone is visiting on a tiny mobile phone or a ginormous desktop monitor - the site will remain easy to navigate and engage with at all times.

Test & Launch

After the complete planning, designing & developing the ideal website, we tested it on multiple mobile, desktop, tab devices to ensure it is universally accessible and seamless experience.

In the entire project, we followed the agile process, in which worked with directors of the studio emerge. This process allowed our team to test and get the effective client feedback at an early stage without any last minute mishaps.

Result

Studio Emerge has been thrilled with their new website, and the improvements to the KPI's we've observed through analytics show that website visitors are finding what they're looking for and engaging with the content on the site.
